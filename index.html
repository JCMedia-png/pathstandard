Why this works when the treadmill did not

Strength first. The fat follows.

Most men over 30 try to cardio their way out of it. They run more, eat less, lose a bit of weight, and end up a smaller version of the same soft body.

What actually changes your shape

Strength training is what builds and keeps muscle while you lose fat. Muscle is what makes you look athletic rather than just lighter, and it is the first thing you lose when you diet without training. That is why the program is built on kettlebells and not on running further.

Why a kettlebell and not a gym

One bell trains the hinge, the squat, the press, the pull and the carry. That is nearly every pattern your body uses in a day. It lives in your garage, it takes 20 minutes, and it is still there at 9pm when the job ran long and the gym is shut.

  • Twenty to forty five minutes, at home, with one kettlebell
  • Endurance work that builds an engine instead of just burning calories
  • Three nutrition rules, no app, no weighing food
  • Walk every day, including the days you do not train

The method

PATH

Purpose, Adventure, Tactical, Health. Three phases over six months, because three months builds the base and the next three is where the transformation actually happens. And because "lose some weight" falls apart in week three, while "be the dad who still gets off the couch at 55" does not.

Phase one, months 1 to 3

Base

Four training days a week, never more, because your job is already physical. Kettlebell strength, kettlebell conditioning, two endurance sessions, and a walk every day. Protein at every meal, three nutrition rules you can follow at a gas station, and real attention on sleep, which is the unglamorous part that decides whether any of the rest works. The goal in phase one is not intensity, it is that you do not miss.

Phase two, months 4 to 6

Transform

Heavier bells, longer and harder endurance, and the part where your clothes stop fitting the way they did. By the end of it a 40 mile Saturday ride is a thing you do for fun, not a thing you train months for.

Ongoing

Brotherhood

The part most programs do not have. A room full of men who own businesses and train. Two live calls a week, quarterly adventure trips, team race access, and an accountability partner who has been in it six months longer than you.

Questions men actually ask

Before you apply

Can I get in great shape after 40, or did I wait too long?

I got abs at 42 after years of drinking every night. Your age is not what has been stopping you. The story you have been telling yourself about your age is.

Can kettlebells really build the body I want?

Yes, and they protect the parts of you your job has been wearing out. Kettlebell work trains the hip hinge, the carry and the press, which is most of what your body actually does all day.

Is 20 to 45 minutes enough?

Four focused sessions a week beats six you skip. You already know what happens when the program needs 90 minutes and your week does not have it.

What if my schedule blows up, or I travel?

Then we shrink the session instead of skipping it. The program serves you, not the other way around. Adjusting a session to the day you actually had is a skill, and it is the one that keeps people here at month six.

What about my knees, back and shoulders?

Every plan starts from what your body can do now. The whole point of training this way is to stop your work from breaking you down.

I know what to do. Why do I need a coach?

You probably do know. You have known for five years. What you have not had is a system built around a 60 hour week and a person who notices when you go quiet.

What does it cost?

The price is fixed and it is the same for every member, so there is no haggling and no custom quotes. I do not publish the number on this page because it only makes sense once you understand what you are actually getting, so if we talk and it is a fit, I will walk you through exactly what it costs on the call, including the payment plan options.

What if I pay and fall off again?

This is the real question, and it is fair. It is why there is a daily check-in, a partner who is six months ahead of you, and two calls a week. The structure is the product.
